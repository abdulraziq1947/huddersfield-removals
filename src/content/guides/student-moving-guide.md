## When to book your student move

Term-time moving dates cluster around the end of semester in December and May/June. Book early, popular weekends fill quickly when thousands of University of Huddersfield students leave halls simultaneously.

### Key dates to plan around

- **End of autumn term**: typically mid-December
- **End of spring term**: May and early June
- **Start of academic year**: September arrivals into new accommodation
- **House share changeovers**: often 1 July when tenancy agreements roll over in Kirklees

## What to take and what to leave

Student rooms accumulate clutter fast. Before packing, decide what is worth transporting.

### Usually worth moving

- Laptops, monitors and electronics (pack carefully, see our fragile packing guidance)
- Bedding, towels and clothes
- Kitchen essentials if moving into an unfurnished house share
- Textbooks and course materials for next semester
- Small furniture you own, desk chair, lamp, mini fridge

### Often better to replace

- Half-used toiletries and cleaning products
- Old lecture notes you will never reread
- Broken or duplicate kitchen items
- Flat-pack furniture that will not survive another assembly, check if dismantling and reassembly is worth the effort

Selling textbooks online or donating to the next tenant reduces volume and lowers your removal quote.

## Packing tips for student moves

You do not need expensive packing materials, but you do need enough boxes and proper protection for electronics.

### Practical packing advice

- **Use small boxes for books**: a large box of textbooks is impossible to lift
- **Wrap electronics** in clothing or bubble wrap; pack cables in labelled bags
- **Bag hanging clothes** in bin liners with the hangers poking through the top, or use wardrobe boxes
- **Label every box** with your name, new address and room (Bedroom, Kitchen)
- **Keep valuables with you**: passport, laptop, phone, wallet and medication travel in your car or bag, not on the van

## Moving from halls of residence

University halls often have strict checkout times and limited parking for loading. Check your accommodation's moving-out checklist:

- Book a loading bay or parking slot if the halls require it
- Schedule your removal crew to arrive after your final room inspection
- Clean the room to avoid deposit deductions, removal day is not an excuse to leave mess
- Confirm lift access and whether trolleys are permitted in corridors

## Moving into a shared house

House shares near the University of Huddersfield, particularly in areas like Ashenhurst, Lockwood and the town centre, often involve narrow stairs, limited parking and multiple tenants moving on the same day.

### Before moving in

- Confirm your tenancy start date and key collection time
- Photograph the property condition for your deposit record
- Check which furniture and appliances the landlord provides, do not move a sofa you do not need
- Coordinate with housemates on who is moving when, to avoid congestion

### Access and parking

Terraced streets around Huddersfield rarely have driveways. Arrange parking with housemates or apply for a temporary suspension through Kirklees Council if you are loading or unloading on yellow lines. Tell your removal company about stairs and narrow doorways at survey so the quote includes adequate crew.

## Moving home for the summer

Many students store belongings at the family home over summer rather than paying for a Kirklees storage unit. If you are moving back to parents in West Yorkshire or beyond:

- Confirm what space is available at home before packing everything
- Declutter aggressively, your childhood bedroom may be smaller than you remember
- Book a removal date that allows travel time if you are moving outside Kirklees
- Consider whether a single trip in a Luton van beats multiple car journeys for cost and convenience

## Budgeting your student move

Student budgets are tight. Legitimate ways to keep costs down:

- **Share a van** with housemates moving to the same area on the same day
- **Pack yourself** using free boxes from supermarkets (reject damaged ones)
- **Move midweek** rather than on the busiest Saturday of term end
- **Declutter** before requesting a quote, less volume means a lower fixed price
- **Book a video survey**: no need to travel back to Huddersfield before the move

Avoid false economies: an uninsured driver damaging your laptop costs far more than a professional removal quote.
