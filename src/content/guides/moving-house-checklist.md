## Eight weeks before moving day

### Confirm dates and book your removal company

Contact your solicitor or estate agent to confirm exchange and completion dates. Once you have a firm moving date, request removal quotes from reputable local firms. A proper quote requires a survey, either in person or by video, so the company can assess volume, access, parking and any items needing dismantling and reassembly.

### Start decluttering

Go through each room and decide what is moving with you. Sell, donate or responsibly dispose of items you no longer need. Decluttering before packing reduces the volume on the van, which can lower your removal costs and simplify loading and unloading at both properties.

### Notify key organisations

Inform your employer, GP surgery, dentist, bank and insurance providers of your change of address. If you have children, contact their schools early. Register with Kirklees Council for council tax at your new address and arrange a final reading for utilities at your current home.

## Four to six weeks before moving day

### Gather packing materials

Order packing materials early: sturdy boxes in mixed sizes, bubble wrap, packing paper, tape, marker pens and wardrobe cartons for hanging clothes. Label every box with its destination room and a brief contents note. Keep fragile items together and mark boxes clearly.

If you prefer not to pack yourself, [Huddersfield Removals packing services](/packing-services/) supply professional-grade materials and trained packers who know how to protect glass, electronics and furniture for transport.

### Arrange change of address services

Set up Royal Mail redirection, it catches post sent to your old address during the transition. Update driving licence details with DVLA and amend vehicle registration if applicable. Notify TV licensing, subscriptions and online retailers.

### Plan access at both properties

Walk through loading and unloading logistics at your current and new home. Note narrow hallways, stairs, lift access, parking restrictions and whether you need a parking suspension from Kirklees Council. Flag any items that require dismantling and reassembly, beds, wardrobes, dining tables and flat-pack furniture, so your removal team can allow time and bring the right tools.

## Two to three weeks before moving day

### Pack room by room

Start with rooms you use least: loft, spare bedroom, garage, shed. Pack books flat in small boxes to keep weight manageable. Leave everyday kitchen items until the final week. Photograph cable setups behind TVs and computers before disconnecting them.

### Confirm your removal booking

Contact Huddersfield Removals to reconfirm your date, start time and any special requirements. Check that your fixed quote covers the agreed services, packing, dismantling, reassembly, number of crew and estimated hours. Ask what is included in your goods in transit insurance and whether you need additional cover for high-value items.

### Arrange childcare and pet care

Moving day is chaotic. Arrange for children and pets to stay with friends or family if possible. This keeps them safe and lets the removal crew work efficiently during loading and unloading.

## One week before moving day

### Finish packing non-essentials

Aim to have 80% of boxes packed by midweek. Prepare a first-night box with kettle, mugs, tea, snacks, phone chargers, toiletries, bedding, torch and basic tools. Keep passports, completion documents, keys and medication in a bag you carry personally, never load these onto the van.

### Defrost and clean

Defrost your freezer at least 24 hours before the move. Clean the oven and appliances you are leaving behind if required by your sale agreement. Take meter readings and photograph them for your records.

### Final utility arrangements

Confirm broadband installation or transfer at your new address. Arrange mail to be held or redirected. Give your new address to friends, family and anyone who needs it on moving day.

## Moving day checklist

### Before the crew arrives

Strip beds except the one you slept in. Do a final sweep of cupboards, loft and garden outbuildings. Ensure clear paths for loading and unloading. Protect floors and banisters if needed. Have cash or card ready for any agreed extras.

When Huddersfield Removals arrives, walk the team through both properties. Point out fragile items, boxes marked "open first" and anything needing extra care. Your crew will load systematically, using blankets and straps to secure furniture in the 3.5-tonne Luton van.

### During the move

Stay reachable by phone. Be present at the new property before unloading begins so you can direct boxes to the correct rooms. Check off items against your inventory if you prepared one. Test smoke alarms and locate stopcock and fuse box at the new house.

### After unloading

Inspect furniture and boxes for damage before the crew leaves. Sign paperwork only when you are satisfied. Assemble beds and essential furniture first. Take meter readings at the new property. Register with a local GP if you have moved within Kirklees or beyond.
