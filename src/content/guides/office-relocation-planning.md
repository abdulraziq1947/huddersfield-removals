## Appoint a move coordinator

Every office relocation needs one person with authority to make decisions. The move coordinator liaises with the removal company, IT support, landlords, and staff.

### Responsibilities

- Maintain the master relocation timeline
- Book the removal survey and confirm the fixed quote
- Communicate move dates and packing instructions to all employees
- Coordinate access at both old and new premises
- Manage change-of-address notifications for clients, suppliers and regulators

Without a single point of contact, office moves stall on unanswered questions about who packs what and which desk goes where.

## Conduct a pre-move audit

Walk both premises before the survey. Note what is moving, what is being replaced, and what requires special handling.

### Inventory categories

- **Desks and chairs**: quantify items needing dismantling and reassembly
- **Filing cabinets**: empty personal files into labelled boxes; lock confidential cabinets
- **IT equipment**: computers, monitors, servers, phones, network hardware
- **Kitchen and breakout areas**: often overlooked until the last day
- **Reception and meeting room furniture**: may need specialist handling
- **Marketing materials and stock**: assess volume for packing materials and van space

Share the audit with your removal company at survey stage so your quote reflects actual volume and access at both Kirklees addresses.

## Plan IT relocation carefully

IT downtime is the most expensive part of most office relocations. Work with your IT provider or internal team on a detailed plan.

### IT move checklist

- **Back up all data** before any equipment is disconnected
- **Photograph cable layouts** behind desks and in server areas
- **Label every cable and device** with desk number or user name
- **Schedule disconnection** after the last working day at the old office
- **Arrange reconnection** at the new premises before staff return
- **Test internet and phone lines** at the new address before move day, lead times for broadband installation in West Yorkshire can be several weeks

Pack monitors and PCs in original boxes where possible, or use professional packing materials with adequate cushioning. Never stack heavy items on top of IT equipment during loading and unloading.

## Communicate with staff early

Staff need clear instructions weeks before the move, not a last-minute email.

### What employees should know

- **Move date and timeline**: including whether they work from home on move day
- **Packing responsibilities**: personal desk items packed by the employee; communal areas handled by the removal team or facilities staff
- **Labelling system**: desk number, department, and new floor location
- **New office layout**: provide a floor plan showing where each team sits
- **Parking and access** at the new building on their first day back

Clear communication reduces confusion during unloading and helps staff settle quickly in the new Kirklees or West Yorkshire premises.

## Choose the right moving schedule

Minimising downtime means loading and unloading when the office is empty.

### Scheduling options

- **Weekend move**: load Friday evening or Saturday, set up Sunday, staff arrive Monday
- **Evening move**: suitable for smaller offices with limited equipment
- **Phased move**: relocate department by department over several days if you cannot close entirely

## Packing materials and labelling for commercial moves

Office contents look uniform, hundreds of similar boxes, so labelling is critical.

### Labelling system

Use a consistent format on every box and item:

- **Department** (e.g. Accounts, Sales)
- **Employee name or desk number**
- **New location** (floor, zone, desk)
- **Contents summary** (e.g. "Monitor + keyboard", "Personal files")

Colour-coded labels by department speed up unloading. Provide staff with packing materials, boxes, tape, markers, at least one week before the move. ## Access and logistics at both premises

Commercial properties in Huddersfield vary widely, modern units with loading bays, converted mills with narrow access, town-centre offices with restricted parking.

### Logistics to confirm

- **Loading bay booking** at both old and new buildings
- **Lift access and weight limits** for furniture and server racks
- **Parking suspensions** from Kirklees Council if needed for the removal van
- **Security and key handover** arrangements with building management
- **Floor protection**: request mats or boards for high-traffic routes during loading and unloading

Share floor plans, access codes and contact details for both building managers with your removal company before move day.

## Legal and administrative tasks

An office relocation triggers administrative work beyond the physical move.

- **Change of address** with Companies House, HMRC, banks and insurers
- **Update website, email footers and Google Business Profile** with the new address
- **Notify clients and suppliers**: send advance notice with the effective date
- **Transfer utilities**: electricity, gas, water, broadband, waste collection
- **Review lease obligations** on the old premises, reinstatement and dilapidations
- **Update fire safety and health and safety documentation** for the new layout

Allow several weeks for registrations and utility transfers in West Yorkshire.

## Post-move setup

The relocation is not complete when the last box is unloaded. Plan the first week in the new office:

- **IT fully operational** before staff arrive
- **Health and safety walkthrough**: fire exits, first aid, display screen equipment setup
- **Furniture reassembly**: desks, meeting tables and reception seating in place
- **Signage updated**: building directory, office door names, visitor directions
- **Staff orientation**: brief tour covering facilities, parking and emergency procedures

A well-planned first day sets the tone for the new premises.
