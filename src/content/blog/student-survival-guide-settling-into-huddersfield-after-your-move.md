The boxes are in, the van has gone, and you're standing in a new room in a new town. The first fortnight sets the tone for the whole year, here's the practical checklist that makes it land well.

## Week one: the admin that can't wait

- **Photograph everything.** Meter readings, existing damage, the state of every room, timestamped photos on day one are your deposit's best friend at the end of the tenancy.
- **Register with a GP.** The University of Huddersfield has a health centre, and town-centre practices take student registrations. Do it while you're healthy, not when you're not.
- **Sort the utilities story.** In most HMOs bills are included; if yours aren't, get accounts set up and submit those meter readings before the first bill guesses for you.
- **Council tax exemption.** Full-time students are exempt, but Kirklees Council needs to know you're a student, your enrolment confirmation handles it. Don't ignore a council tax letter; respond with your exemption.
- **Contents insurance.** Check whether a parent's policy covers you; if not, student contents policies cost little and laptops are what thieves like.

## Week two: learn the town

Huddersfield rewards a wander. The essentials for orientation:

- **The station** is one of the finest railway buildings in the country and the gateway to Leeds and Manchester in under 40 minutes each, handy for placements and weekends.
- **The market and town centre** cover cheap produce and everything a student kitchen needs.
- **Greenhead Park** is the default green space for running, revising outdoors and pretending winter isn't coming.
- **Bus routes** radiate from the town centre to every student area; a term pass usually beats paying per ride.

## Make the room yours

Unpack fully in the first week, a lived-in room beats a box fort for morale. Command strips for posters (landlords notice Blu-Tack), a decent desk lamp, and bedding from home do more for homesickness than anything else.
