Every summer we meet customers rescuing a move that went wrong with someone else, the no-show van, the quote that doubled on the day, the sofa that travelled unwrapped. Almost all of it is avoidable with ten minutes of vetting. Here's the checklist.

## Do: ask about insurance, specifically

Any professional firm can tell you two numbers without hesitation: goods-in-transit cover and public liability. Ours are **£50,000 per vehicle load** and **£1,000,000** respectively, written into our terms and conditions. If a company can't or won't state these figures, that's your answer.

## Do: get the quote in writing

A verbal "should be about £300" is not a quote; it's an opening position. A written quote states the price, what's included (dismantling? packing? mileage?), and what would change it. Ours are valid for three months.

## Do: read reviews on a platform the company can't edit

Google reviews attached to a business profile can't be curated by the business. Read the recent ones and the bad ones, how a company responds to criticism tells you more than the five-star average.

## Do: check what happens if things go wrong

Cancellation charges, claims deadlines, damage procedures, professionals publish this in their terms. Under ours, for example, damage must be noted at handover and missing items reported within 24 hours. Knowing the rules before moving day protects both sides.

## Don't: book on price alone

The cheapest quote usually excludes something: insurance, a second crew member, fuel, VAT. When one quote is dramatically lower than the rest, the difference is what's missing from it.

## Don't: assume "man and van" equals removals company

A man with a van is fine for a mattress across town. A house move needs trained crew, blankets, straps, dollies, insurance and a plan for your four-poster and the fridge-freezer. They're different products at different prices for a reason.

## Don't: leave booking to the last week

Good local firms book out first, especially month-end Fridays and June. Two to three weeks' notice gets you the date and time you actually want.

## The two-minute test

Call the firm. Do they ask about access, floors, parking and what's being moved? Professionals scope; cowboys just say yes. Try it on us: 01484 212450, seven days a week.
