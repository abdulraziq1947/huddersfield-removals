An office move is a project with a payroll attached: every hour the business is offline costs money. The good news is that office moves are predictable, the same handful of decisions determine whether Monday morning works or doesn't.

## Eight weeks out: set the sequence

- Appoint one internal move coordinator, a single point of contact halves the confusion.
- Confirm the new floor plan and label it: every desk, room and storage run gets a number.
- Book the removals company for an evening or weekend slot so working hours survive. We run most office moves Friday night to Sunday.
- Notify your landlord, insurer, and anyone who needs building access arrangements at either end.

## Four weeks out: tackle the IT question

Physical transport of workstations, monitors and printers is our job, wrapped, boxed and labelled to the floor plan. Disconnection and reconnection of live infrastructure is your IT provider's. Book them for the same weekend now; IT availability is the most common office-move bottleneck we see.

Meanwhile: update the address everywhere it lives: Google Business Profile, website, invoices, bank, Companies House if registered there.

## One week out: crates and labels

- Every employee packs their own desk into a labelled crate, one crate per person keeps unpacking sane.
- Purge as you pack. Office moves are the best filing amnesty you'll ever get; confidential waste goes to a shredding service, not the van.
- Label everything with its **destination** desk number, not its origin.

## Moving weekend

A well-planned office move runs in sequence: furniture and storage first, crates second, IT last off and first on. With desks rebuilt and crates on the right desks by Sunday afternoon, Monday is just plugging in.
