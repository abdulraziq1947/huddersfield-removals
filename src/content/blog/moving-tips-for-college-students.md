Student moves are small but spiky: hard deadlines, deposit inspections, narrow staircases and everything happening in the same two weeks as everyone else's move. Here's how to make yours boring, in the good way.

## Know your dates, then book early

Huddersfield's student calendar concentrates moves into late June (tenancy end) and September (arrival). Vans and crews book out fastest in exactly those weeks. As soon as you know your tenancy end date, book the move, waiting until the week itself is how you end up carrying a mattress across town.

## Packing a student room in an afternoon

- **Clothes**: bin bags are fine for the van; wardrobe cartons if you care about creases.
- **Books and files**: small boxes only, they get heavy fast.
- **Kitchen kit**: wrap plates in tea towels, jars sealed in a zip bag. It's one box; don't overthink it.
- **Electronics**: original boxes if you kept them; otherwise bubble wrap and a "FRAGILE" label. Back up the laptop before it travels.
- **The paperwork folder**: tenancy agreement, deposit protection details, meter photos. Keep it with you, not in the van.

## Protect the deposit

Before you leave: photograph every room, every meter reading, and the state of anything the inventory mentions. Do it after the room is empty, an empty, clean room in photos is the evidence that ends most deposit disputes.
