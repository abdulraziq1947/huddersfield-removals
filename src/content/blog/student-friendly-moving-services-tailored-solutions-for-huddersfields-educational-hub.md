With the University of Huddersfield sitting right on Queensgate, thousands of students churn through halls, house shares and studios every academic year. Student moves are their own discipline, small loads, hard deadlines, awkward buildings, and they deserve a service shaped around that, not a scaled-down house move.

## What makes a student move different

**The load is small.** A typical student room is boxes, bedding, a bike, maybe a desk chair and a TV. That's a part-van load, and it should be priced like one, not like a family move.

**The buildings are awkward.** Huddersfield's student housing is dominated by converted terraces and purpose-built blocks around Aspley, Springwood and the town centre. Narrow Victorian staircases, top-floor rooms, no lifts, nowhere to park. Crews that work these streets weekly know which doors need the sofa-angle trick and where a van can legally wait.

**The deadlines are absolute.** Tenancies end when they end. Checkout inspections, key returns and deposit protection all hang on being out on time, which is why we schedule student moves with buffer, not optimism.

## The three moves of student life

1. **Into halls or a first house share**: usually a parent's car job, honestly. Where we help: bulky items, group moves, and long-distance arrivals from elsewhere in the UK.
2. **Between houses within Huddersfield**: the classic June move. Same-day, short-distance, often shareable with housemates moving nearby. This is our [short-distance service](/short-distance-moves/) at its most efficient.
3. **Home at the end of it all**: a loaded, insured van to anywhere on the UK mainland beats a convoy of overloaded hatchbacks. Book this one early; end-of-term weeks fill first.

## Keeping it affordable

- Share a van with housemates heading the same direction.
- Be packed and ready, student moves are quick when the room is boxed.
- Move midweek if your tenancy allows.
- Skip the packing service for a single room; spend the savings on decent boxes instead, [we sell them](/packing-materials/).
